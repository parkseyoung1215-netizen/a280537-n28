# Devlog

## 2026-10-22

AI startups that use AI cover a very wide range, and they are making people's lives more convenient. But I kept thinking about what I could do to actually help people in a practical way, and that is how I became interested in this field and started researching it. The result was a small amount: about 75 euros a year for one household. This made me wonder whether this convenience would really help people. I think that if this is combined on a larger scale, much more money could be saved, so I plan to do broader research next.

## 2026-10-22 (conclusion)

Even when I grouped many homes together, I did not see any meaningful savings. Seeing this, I thought it would still help to some extent, but I felt that I could not be satisfied with such a small amount of help. I wanted to give people big help or make their lives easier, but I do not think I would find that even if the experiments continued, so I am stopping here. While doing this project, I felt how far technology in the world has developed, and at the same time I realized once again how hard it is to discover something new that can help people.
