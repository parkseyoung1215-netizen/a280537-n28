# Devlog

## 2026-10-23

I started this project because, as the world develops, an enormous variety of advanced technologies is pouring out indiscriminately, and various policies and conflicts keep arising. In this situation, I wondered whether I could read the future through AI, so I decided to try it. While building it, I changed the fixed frame of answers and questions so that it could handle many directions, in order to check the results of various situations. I also felt that events with no precedent or with a low chance of happening need to be controlled. Next, I plan to verify it, develop it further, use it together with various AI agents, and use it as my own tool.
